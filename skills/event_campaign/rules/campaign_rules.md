# event_campaign rules

1. **Date accuracy** — copy event dates verbatim; never shift, reformat
   into ambiguity, or add a year that was not given.
2. **Ticket link** — include exactly once per post when provided; never
   shorten or alter it.
3. **Cadence** — announce ≥7 days out, reminder 2–3 days out, last-call
   day-of. Do not compress all three into one day.
4. **No fake sellout** — scarcity claims require real capacity info.
5. **Accessibility** — include venue/location in every post of the series.
